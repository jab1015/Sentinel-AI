# Sentinel AI Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026  
**Provider:** Modern Methods, United States

Sentinel AI is a Windows system monitoring, diagnostic, security, and assistance application provided by Modern Methods.

## 1. Information Sentinel AI Processes

Sentinel AI processes information about the Windows computer on which it is installed in order to provide monitoring, diagnostics, security analysis, troubleshooting, and repair assistance.

Depending on the feature being used, this information may include system configuration, operating system information, hardware and device information, drivers, running processes, network and connection information, security status, diagnostic results, Windows events and logs, application-generated diagnostic information, and other technical evidence relevant to identifying or resolving a computer problem.

## 2. Local Processing

Sentinel AI is designed to perform monitoring and evidence collection locally whenever practical. Local monitoring does not require information to be sent to an external AI service simply because Sentinel AI is running.

## 3. Cloud AI Investigations

When Sentinel AI determines that additional analysis is needed and an eligible cloud AI feature is used, relevant technical evidence and the user's question may be securely transmitted to Sentinel AI's cloud service for analysis.

Sentinel AI is designed to send information relevant to the investigation rather than intentionally transmitting unrelated personal files or document contents.

Cloud AI processing may use third-party service providers, including OpenAI, to analyze submitted diagnostic information and generate an investigation or assistance response.

Information transmitted to service providers is subject to the applicable provider agreements and privacy protections used by Modern Methods.

## 4. Microsoft Store Subscription Information

Sentinel AI may use Microsoft Store licensing services to determine whether a valid Sentinel AI subscription is active.

Purchases and subscription billing are processed by Microsoft. Sentinel AI does not receive or store the customer's complete payment card or banking information.

## 5. Information Sentinel AI Does Not Intentionally Collect

Sentinel AI is not designed to collect or sell personal information for advertising purposes.

Sentinel AI does not intentionally collect the contents of personal documents, photographs, email messages, passwords, payment card numbers, or other unrelated personal content as part of ordinary system monitoring.

Technical information may occasionally contain identifying information, such as computer, account, device, file, process, network, or path names. Sentinel AI attempts to limit external processing to information relevant to the requested diagnostic or security function.

## 6. Data Use

Information processed by Sentinel AI is used to provide and improve functions including:

- system monitoring;
- security and diagnostic analysis;
- identification of computer problems;
- investigation of technical or security conditions;
- repair recommendations and verified repair workflows;
- Microsoft Store subscription verification;
- reliability, troubleshooting, and service operation.

Modern Methods does not sell Sentinel AI user information.

## 7. Data Retention

Local diagnostic information may remain on the user's computer as part of Sentinel AI logs, history, configuration, quarantine, or other application data until removed through the application, Windows, or application uninstallation, as applicable.

Information sent for cloud processing may be temporarily processed or retained as necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and maintain the service and as required by applicable service-provider policies or law.

Modern Methods seeks to minimize unnecessary retention of machine-specific diagnostic information.

## 8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information transmitted to Sentinel AI cloud services. No computer system or Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. User Control

Users can choose whether to use Sentinel AI's subscription-based cloud AI capabilities. Local Sentinel functionality that does not require cloud AI may continue to operate without an active cloud AI subscription where supported.

Sentinel AI does not authorize potentially disruptive system changes solely because an AI system recommends them. Repair actions are subject to Sentinel AI's verification and safety controls.

## 10. Children

Sentinel AI is a computer security and diagnostic utility and is not directed toward children.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Sentinel AI features, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change. Updated versions will identify a revised effective date.

## 12. Contact

Privacy questions regarding Sentinel AI may be directed to Modern Methods through the support contact information provided with Sentinel AI or its Microsoft Store listing.
